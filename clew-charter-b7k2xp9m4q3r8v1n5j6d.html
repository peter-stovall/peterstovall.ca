The Grand & Perpetuall Charter
of the Most Honourable Clew of Bookworms
❧

We, the Worms of Sound Mind and Segmented Body, burrowing forth from the Good Earth in pursuit of Knowledge, Camaraderie, and the turning of Pages most fyne, do hereby ordain and establish this Charter for the governance of our Clew. Let it be known unto all Creatures, invertebrate and otherwise, that this Compact shall bind us in literary fellowship, that we might read together, discourse with vigour, and burrow ever deeper into the rich soil of Letters. So say we all, from head to whichever end we happen to be facing.

❧
Article the First
Of Membership & the Counting of Worms
Section I.

The membership of this Clew shall be limited to Eight (8) Worms of Good Standing, and not one segment more. This number, being just and proper for vigorous discourse yet intimate enough for true fellowship, shall not be exceeded save by amendment to this Charter as described herein.

Section II. — On the Matter of Amputation.

In the unfortunate & lamentable event that a Worm be sundered in twain — whether by spade, bird, or misadventure — that Worm shall be obliged to declare, within a fortnight of said severing, which half shall retain membership in this Clew. The unchosen half shall be regarded henceforth as a separate Worm entirely, possessed of no claim to the seat, the reading list, or the snacks. Let there be no ambiguity: one worm, one vote; one half, one farewell.

Article the Second
Of Good Standing & the Duties of a Diligent Worm
Section I.

Each Worm shall maintain Good Standing at all times. A Worm shall be deemed to be in Good Standing if, and only if, the following conditions be met in their entirety:

(i) The Worm hath attended more than fifty per centum (50%) of all Clew Gatherings upon a rolling basis of six calendar months; and
(ii) The Worm hath not been absent from more than two (2) consecutive Clew Gatherings without having declared Good Cause no fewer than three (3) weeks in advance of each respective Gathering so missed.
Section II. — On the Nature of Good Cause.

Good Cause shall be understood to mean any of the following, & such like circumstances as a Worm of sound judgement would consider sufficient:

(a) Travel for Work or Pleasure, be it through soil foreign or domestic;
(b) Injury or Illness, accompanied by verbal or visual proof satisfactory to the Clew — a pale complexion, a crooked segment, or a physician's note (if Worm physicians do exist) shall suffice;
(c) Any other cause that a Worm of reasonable temperament and good character would deem just and proper. The Clew shall interpret this clause with generosity of spirit, but not with foolishness.
Article the Third
Of Ejection & the Casting Out of Wayward Worms
Section I.

Any Worm who doth fail, at any point in time, to satisfy the conditions of Good Standing as set forth in Article the Second shall be ipso facto and without further ceremony removed from this Clew. There shall be no appeal, no wriggling out of it, and no hard feelings — for the Charter is the Charter, and even a Worm must live by a Code.

Section II.

The ejected Worm shall be notified of their removal by such means as the Clew deems fit — be it a solemn message, a gentle tap on the segment, or a note left in their burrow. The ejected Worm may, at some future date, be considered for readmission under the provisions of Article the Fourth, should a vacancy arise and the Clew be so inclined.

Article the Fourth
Of Selection & the Filling of Empty Wormholes
Section I.

When a Worm doth perish (may they rest in compost), is cast out by the terms of this Charter, or doth voluntarily de-Worm — that is, withdraw of their own free will from the fellowship — the Clew may, at its next scheduled Gathering, vote upon a replacement to fill the empty Wormhole.

Section II.

Any Worm in Good Standing may nominate a candidate. The nominee must be of sound character, literary curiosity, and willing to abide by this Charter in its entirety. A simple majority of Worms present at the Gathering shall suffice to admit the new member, who shall thereupon enjoy all rights, responsibilities, and reading assignments pertaining to membership in this Clew.

Done in Convention, by the Unanimous Consent of the Worms present,
in the Year of our Soil, Two Thousand and Twenty-Six.
