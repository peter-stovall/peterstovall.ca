Per the Charter as amended: Good Standing requires attending 5 or more of the last 8 Gatherings within the current cycle, and not being absent more than 2 consecutive Gatherings without a Good Cause declared at least 4 weeks in advance for each. Good Cause itself is always the Clew's judgment call — this page only checks the objective facts (was something declared, and was it on time) and shows its work so you can check its reading against your own.

Modeling assumption to flag: the charter's consecutive-absence clause is grammatically ambiguous about whether a run of 3+ consecutive absences needs Good Cause on every absence in the run, or only the ones beyond the first two. This page takes the stricter reading (every absence in a run longer than 2 needs its own valid, timely declaration) since that's the more defensible drafting convention — but it's a genuine judgment call, and the per-gathering breakdown below lets you re-check it under the looser reading if the Clew prefers.
