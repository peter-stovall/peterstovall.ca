About

With 12+ years of global experience across five countries and a background as a qualified lawyer, Peter brings a unique blend of regulatory expertise, technical depth, and product leadership to every challenge. He has launched products spanning clean energy, cybersecurity, mining, M&A, intellectual property, litigation, food delivery, and corporate education.

As Head of Product for Raptor Comply at Raptor Maps, Peter launched a B2B cybersecurity compliance platform for renewable energy, owning sales, marketing, product, and engineering. Previously, he drove 11x revenue growth as Director of Product Management, contributing to a successful $35M Series C.

Peter established product organizations and new revenue streams at two global law firms (Fasken and Freshfields), earning recognition as Canada's and Europe's most innovative firms. He pioneers AI-native product practices—ranked in the top 1% globally on Lovable for AI prototyping.

His passion lies at the intersection of regulatory complexity and product innovation. He embraces regulatory shifts as early product opportunities, building solutions that solve real operational problems and advance climate-critical missions.
