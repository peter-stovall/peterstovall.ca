About

Peter Stovall is a 0-to-1-to-100 product leader and qualified lawyer with 12+ years launching products across energy, cybersecurity, legal tech, and compliance. He pioneers AI-native product practices—ranked in the top 1% globally on Lovable for AI prototyping.

As Head of Product for Raptor Comply at Raptor Maps, Peter launched a B2B cybersecurity compliance platform for renewable energy in under 3 months, securing a paid pilot that converted to the first customer at launch. He now owns sales, marketing, product, and engineering with a $1M annual budget.

Previously, Peter drove 11x revenue growth as Director of Product Management at Raptor Maps, contributing to a successful $35M Series C. He established product organizations and new revenue streams at two global law firms (Fasken and Freshfields), earning recognition as Canada's and Europe's most innovative firms.

As a qualified lawyer, Peter embraces regulatory shifts as early product opportunities. He builds products that solve real operational problems and advance climate-critical missions.
